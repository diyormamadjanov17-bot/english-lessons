Lesson 1 — Overview
What is the Present Simple?
We use this tense for things that are always or usually true.

🌍 Facts & Truths

The sun rises in the east.
Water boils at 100°C.

🔄 Habits & Routines

She goes to school every day.
I drink coffee in the morning.

📅 Schedules

The train leaves at 9 AM.
The shop opens at 8.

🕐 Signal Words

always, usually, often, sometimes,
never, every day, on Mondays
Lesson 2 — Structure
How to Build the Sentence
Three forms: positive, negative, and question.
✅ Subject + verb (base / +s)
She plays tennis.  |  They play tennis.
❌ Subject + do/does not + verb
She doesn't play.  |  They don't play.
❓ Do/Does + Subject + verb?
Does she play?  |  Do they play?
he / she / it → +s or +es
He reads. She watches. It goes.
Use does in negatives & questions
I / you / we / they → base
I read. You watch. They go.
Use do in negatives & questions
Lesson 3 — Spelling
Spelling Rules for He/She/It
Adding -s is not always simple — here are the rules.
Most verbs → +s
work → works
eat → eats, drink → drinks
-s/-sh/-ch/-x/-o → +es
watch → watches
go → goes, fix → fixes, wash → washes
consonant + y → -ies
study → studies
fly → flies, try → tries
Irregular verbs
have → has
be → is  |  do → does
  • She studies English every evening.
  • He goes to the gym on Mondays.
  • My cat has green eyes.
Lesson 4 — Examples
Positive, Negative, Question
See how the same idea changes form.
  • ✅ She works at a hospital.
  • ❌ She doesn't work at a school.
  • ❓ Does she work at a hospital?
  • ✅ They play football on Saturdays.
  • ❌ They don't play basketball.
  • ❓ Do they play football?
  • ✅ I wake up at 7 every morning.
  • ❌ I don't wake up at 5.
  • ❓ Do you wake up early?
